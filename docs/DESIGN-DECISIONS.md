# Design decisions: Habit Tracker (working title)

Decisions log of `docs/DESIGN.md` (moved out of section 9 to keep the design
document short). Each row states the choice, the options considered and the
reason.

**Rule of ownership:** user-visible behavior and its rules live in
`docs/REQUIREMENTS.md` section 7 (IDs `BHV-*`); `docs/DESIGN.md` owns the
structure and mechanisms (components, ports, data model) and `docs/UX.md` the
screens. A row here states the choice and the reasoning and points to the owner
instead of repeating the rule. Decision numbers are stable and referenced from the
other documents.

Decisions 1-39 were approved with the design on 2026-11-08. Later decisions
are listed after them. Numbers 15, 19, 31, 39, 42, 52, 62, 67, 68 and 69 are
unused: those decisions were replaced (by Decision 41 or by `docs/REQUIREMENTS.md`
section 7) and removed.

Decisions 53-72 were agreed with the user on 2026-11-11, after the reviews in
`docs/reviews/`; Decisions 73-81 on 2026-11-13, with the revision of the
requirements; Decisions 82-85 on 2026-11-16, in the UX design. All await approval together with the revised design. A
decision that a later one changed is rewritten in place: the table states
only what is currently valid, with no revision history.

| 11 | The week starts on Monday. | 12 | A duration entry always has a start instant and a length. | 14 | Entries per habit per day: duration many; numeric many, summed; binary at most one (invariant). | Numeric: one overwritten value per day; binary: several per day. | Summing keeps logging low-friction (0.5 l now, 0.3 l later). | 16 | Goal evaluation and percentage as in BHV-EVAL-R1 to R3. | 17 | Habit management: archiving keeps entries and statistics (available for archived habits) and the habit can be restored; deleting removes everything after confirmation; direction and measurement type immutable after creation (Decision 77); name editable; changing a numeric unit only relabels, with a warning, no conversion. | 18 | Several timers may run at once, at most one per duration habit. | (a) one timer at a time; (b) several timers. | (a) adds a rule "what happens on starting a second one", which is friction (key quality). The model already allows (b). (User decision.) |

| 41 | Statistics presets: this week, previous week, this month, previous month, this quarter, this year, 6 months, 1 year (the last two end today, so they do not align with period boundaries; see Decision 55), plus a custom range; default "this week". Rules: BHV-STATS-R1, R5, R6. | Presets: (a) calendar presets; (b) rolling 7 / 30 days with overlapping periods counted; (c) rolling 7 / 30 days, no change. | 43 | Statistics are clipped to the life of the habit: the average per day divides only by days from the creation day (or the earliest entry, if earlier) to the archiving day or today; goal periods count only where a goal applies and the habit is not archived. | 44 | A Binary entry stores a local date, not an instant. | 50 | Day screen on a date other than today: (1) the timer works only on today's date, other dates have manual entry only; (2) the default time of a Numeric entry added on another date is decided in `docs/UX.md` (BHV-ENTRY-R8); (3) the progress indicator shows the period containing the selected date.
