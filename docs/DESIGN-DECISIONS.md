# Design decisions: Habit Tracker (working title)

Decisions log of `docs/DESIGN.md` (moved out of section 9 to keep the design
document short). Each row states the choice, the options considered and the
reason.

**Rule of ownership:** the rules themselves live in `docs/DESIGN.md`
(behavior and rules in section 5, user interface in section 6, invariants in
section 4). A row here states the choice and the reasoning; where a rule is
owned by the design document, the row points to it instead of repeating it.
Decision numbers are stable and referenced from the design document.

Decisions 1-39 were approved with the design on 2026-11-08. Later decisions
are listed after them. Numbers 19 and 39 are unused: those decisions were
replaced by Decision 41 and removed.

Decisions 52-72 were agreed with the user on 2026-11-11, after the reviews in
`docs/reviews/`, and await approval together with the revised design. A
decision that a later one changed is rewritten in place: the table states
only what is currently valid, with no revision history.

| 52 | A goal is a separate entity: a reference to the habit, a target, a period, the first day of its validity and an optional end of validity (calendar days).
